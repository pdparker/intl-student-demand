⚠️ Read the numbers with these caveats
  • These are visa GRANTS, not applications and not enrolments. A grant means a subclass 500 student visa was issued — it is a demand-realised signal (who was let in), one step removed from who applied and one step further from who enrolled or commenced study.
  • Primary applicants only. Dependants (partners, children — secondary applicants) travelling with the student are excluded from every count on this page.
  • National / sector / source-country only — no institution or provider detail. Home Affairs publishes the provider's state but not individual providers, so there is no institution-level or ACU view here. This is macro demand intelligence, not a provider dashboard.
  • 2025-26 is a partial year (to 31 May 2026 only) — shown dashed throughout and never compared directly against a full financial year's total.
  • Policy context. The 2022-23 peak (~500k) was the post-COVID rebound; the fall since reflects tightened settings — Ministerial Direction 107, then 111, higher financial and English-language requirements, and National Planning Level / provider caps from 2025.
  • Complements, but does not join, CRICOS. CRICOS is course supply with no nationality data; this is visa demand with no course/provider data. They are separate datasets presented side by side — a demand×supply join is a possible future dashboard, not this one.
